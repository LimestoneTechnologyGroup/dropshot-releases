Screenshots that land where they belong.

Capture a region, a window or a screen, or record a video. Dropshot sends it straight to a Teams chat, Claude, an email or a folder, using presets you set up once.

Download for Windows Windows 10 and 11 · no admin rights needed · updates itself
Getting started

Install in a minute

  1. Run Dropshot.exe from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose Yes to install. It's added to the Start menu, starts when you sign in, and keeps itself up to date.

For IT

Install silently for the signed-in user with Dropshot.exe --install. To manage versions yourself, set "DisableUpdates": true in %APPDATA%\Dropshot\presets.json.

Everyday use

Keys

KeyWhat it does
PrtScCapture a region. Drag a box, click for one monitor, or press Enter for all of them.
Alt + PrtScCapture the window you're in.
Ctrl + PrtScCapture the screen your mouse is on.
Ctrl + Alt + Shift + PrtScCapture all screens.
Shift + PrtScCopy the link under your mouse, or the browser's page address.
Ctrl + Shift + PrtScStart or stop recording a video (MP4).
Ctrl + Alt + PrtScPause or resume the recording.

Change any of them with Keys… in the tray menu. Windows-key shortcuts like Win + S work too.

Presets

Set it up once, then one key does the rest

Pick after every capture

A small menu lists your presets: press 1–9, click away for your Default, or press Esc to just copy it.

Teams and Claude

Paste straight into a Teams chat by name, link or email address, or into Claude. Email a Teams channel through Outlook.

Mark it up

Arrows, boxes, text, numbered steps, a highlighter, blur for anything private, and crop, all before it's sent.

Your own hotkeys

Give a preset its own key, like "send to the Development Team chat", and it captures and sends in one press.

Nothing leaves your PC unless a preset sends it. Dropshot only goes online to check for its own updates.
