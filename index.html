Dropshot

Screenshots that go straight where you want them: a Teams channel, Claude, an email or a folder. One key, your presets.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 and 11 · no admin rights needed · updates itself

Install

  1. Run Dropshot.exe from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose Yes to install. It's added to your Start menu, starts when you sign in, and keeps itself up to date.

Use

PrtScDrag a box. Click for one monitor, Enter for all of them.
Alt + PrtScThe window you're in.
Ctrl + PrtScAll monitors.
Shift + PrtScSame box as last time.

After each capture, pick a preset with 1–9. Build your own from the tray icon's Presets… window, and give your favourites their own hotkey so they run in one press:

  • Send to Claude: pastes the screenshot into Claude.
  • Post to a Teams channel: opens the channel and pastes it in.
  • Email to a Teams channel: sends it through Outlook to the channel's email address.
  • Save, shrink, border, stamp: in any order you like.

Nothing leaves your PC unless a preset sends it. Dropshot only goes online to check for its own updates.
